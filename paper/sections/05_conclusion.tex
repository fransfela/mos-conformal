\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Feature-space distance separates some corpus shifts, but its link to prediction error is weak and can reverse, so Mahalanobis-stratified intervals help some predictor--domain pairs and harm others. This dissociation also holds on an independent corpus. Fixed split conformal intervals give matched-domain coverage under exchangeability, but arbitrary shift has no coverage guarantee. Beyond the UTMOS scorer-fidelity gap noted in Section~\ref{ssec:setup}, the wrapper assumes calibration-test exchangeability that we do not test here and bins on a fixed Gaussian distance rather than a learned difficulty score. Testing exchangeability online and replacing Mahalanobis distance with a learned, calibrated difficulty estimator are natural next steps.
